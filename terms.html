Knoodle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

At Knoodle, we believe learning should be about gaining knowledge, not spending as much time as possible inside an app. The same principle applies to your personal data. We only want to use information that is needed to provide the learning experience, save your progress, and keep your account working.

This Privacy Policy explains how Knoodle collects, stores, uses, and protects your information. It also explains what control you have over your data, including how you can permanently delete your account.

Knoodle is currently in beta. Features and technical details may change as development continues, but privacy and responsible data handling remain important parts of the project.

Please read this policy carefully so you understand how your information is handled when you use Knoodle.

1. About Knoodle

Knoodle is a free learning platform designed to help people learn and remember knowledge. It starts with language learning and is intended to expand into other areas, such as school subjects, science, coding, technology, and personal study material.

To provide its current online features, Knoodle uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data storage. This allows users to create accounts and access their saved learning progress.

Knoodle is developed independently and is not intended to sell user information or use personal data as an advertising product.

Our principles are straightforward:

  • Your information should only be used for legitimate purposes connected to the service.
  • Your private learning material should remain private.
  • Your password should not be accessible to Knoodle's developers in readable form.
  • You should have control over your account and saved information.
  • You should be able to delete your account and associated data through the app.
  • The basic learning experience should remain free.

This policy applies to the Knoodle application, its account system, and the associated services used to provide its features.

Where applicable, the rights and obligations of the parties are determined by the relevant data protection legislation and the actual services being provided.

3. What information does Knoodle collect?

Knoodle collects information needed to operate your account and provide its learning features. The exact information stored depends on which features you use.

3.1 Account information

When you register, you need to provide a valid email address. Your account may also contain:

  • Your email address.
  • Your username.
  • Your nickname or display name.
  • An internal account identifier used to associate information with your account.
  • Authentication-related information managed by Supabase Auth.

Your email address helps identify your account and supports account-related functions. Your username and nickname help identify you within the application.

An internal account identifier allows Knoodle to associate your study decks and progress with the correct account without relying only on your email address.

3.2 Learning information

Knoodle is designed to remember your progress so you can continue learning where you left off.

Depending on the available features, this may include:

  • Your vocabulary lists and study decks.
  • Questions, answers, definitions, and other study material you create.
  • Exercise answers and results.
  • Your learning progress.
  • Information about which material you have practised.
  • Information used to determine which topics may need further review.
  • Learning settings and preferences.
  • Other data necessary for the app's learning features.

This information exists to make the platform useful. Without saved learning data, Knoodle would not be able to provide the same continuous learning experience across sessions.

Your study material is not intended to be publicly visible simply because it is stored online.

3.3 Technical information

The app and its infrastructure may process limited technical information needed to provide authentication, connect to the database, maintain sessions, and troubleshoot technical problems.

Depending on the services and configuration involved, technical information may include request metadata, timestamps, error details, or security-related logs.

Supabase may also process technical information as part of providing its infrastructure.

Knoodle does not claim that no technical information is ever processed. Rather, the intention is to avoid unnecessary tracking and collect only what is appropriate for operating the service.

3.4 Information we do not intentionally collect

Knoodle is not designed to collect your personal information for advertising profiles, sell your study habits, or build a profile of your interests for unrelated commercial purposes.

We do not ask for your password to be manually entered into a separate Knoodle database.

We also do not currently use AI to generate learning material or write the application's code.

4. How passwords and authentication work

Account security is particularly important because your account contains your saved learning progress.

Knoodle uses Supabase Auth to handle authentication. Password management is handled by the authentication service rather than by a separate password storage system built into Knoodle.

The Knoodle developers do not have access to your password in readable form. We do not store readable passwords in the application's own learning tables.

Authentication systems use security mechanisms to verify users without exposing their passwords as ordinary readable account information.

You should still protect your account by using a strong, unique password and keeping your login details private.

Do not share your password with anyone. If you believe someone else has accessed your account, contact us at kaier.chang@proton.me.

Although we take account security seriously, the use of an authentication provider does not eliminate every possible security risk. You should contact us if you notice suspicious activity or believe your account has been compromised.

6. How Knoodle uses your information

We use your information for purposes connected to providing and maintaining the learning platform.

These purposes include:

Providing your account. Your account information allows you to register, log in, and use Knoodle's account-based features.

Saving your progress. Your learning information allows you to continue studying without having to start from the beginning every time you open the app.

Providing learning features. Study decks, answers, results, and related information help the app provide the features you choose to use.

Maintaining security. Authentication and access controls help prevent unauthorised access to accounts and private learning data.

Maintaining and improving the service. Where necessary, technical information may help us identify errors, fix bugs, and keep the platform working correctly.

Responding to requests. We may use relevant account information to help with account problems, privacy requests, or other support enquiries.

We do not need to track how many hours you spend studying to make Knoodle useful. The goal is to help you remember what you learn, rather than encourage you to spend as much time as possible inside the app.

We do not intend to use your learning history for unrelated advertising or sell it to third parties.

Depending on the particular processing activity, Knoodle may rely on the following legal bases:

Performance of a contract. Certain information is necessary to create your account and provide the features you request, including saving and retrieving your learning progress.

Legitimate interests. Some limited processing may be necessary to maintain the security, stability, and functionality of the platform, provided those interests do not override your rights and freedoms.

Legal obligations. Certain information may need to be retained or disclosed where applicable law requires it.

Consent. Where a particular feature or processing activity legally requires consent, it should be based on appropriate consent rather than assumed permission.

The applicable legal basis depends on the specific purpose. We aim to use personal information only where there is an appropriate reason to do so.

You may have the right to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. This does not make processing that already took place before withdrawal unlawful.

8. Private data and database security

Knoodle uses Row Level Security (RLS) in its Supabase database.

RLS allows database access to be restricted according to defined policies. In Knoodle's intended setup, these policies help ensure that users can access only the data they are authorised to access.

For example, a user should not be able to retrieve another user's private learning progress simply by changing an account identifier in a request.

RLS is an important part of the security setup, but it is not a guarantee by itself. Its effectiveness depends on the policies applied to each relevant table, the permissions granted to database roles, and how the application accesses the database.

Knoodle aims to follow security practices such as:

  • Restricting access to private information.
  • Keeping administrative credentials and secret keys out of public client-side code.
  • Using authentication to identify the account making a request.
  • Applying appropriate database permissions and RLS policies.
  • Avoiding unnecessary exposure of personal information.
  • Correcting security issues when they are discovered.

We want your private study material and progress to be available to you, not to other users.

Supabase processes information to provide authentication, database hosting, and related services.

As with other infrastructure providers, access by authorised personnel or subprocessors may be possible under limited circumstances, subject to the provider's policies, security measures, and applicable law.

Other users

Your private account information and study data are not intended to be available to other users.

Database access policies and application permissions are used to restrict access. However, information you deliberately share publicly, if such a feature is introduced, may be visible to others according to that feature's design.

10. We do not sell your data

Knoodle is designed as a free learning platform, not as a service that earns money by selling user information.

We do not sell your email address, username, nickname, learning progress, or private study material to advertisers.

We do not intend to use your learning data to create advertising profiles or provide unrelated third parties with your personal information for their own marketing.

If information must be shared with a service provider to operate Knoodle, that processing is different from selling your information. Such providers may process data only within the scope of the services and applicable arrangements.

The fact that Knoodle is free does not mean your personal information is intended to be the price you pay.

11. Cookies, sessions, and tracking

Knoodle may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies where necessary to maintain login sessions and provide its features.

For example, authentication tokens or session information may be used to keep you signed in and allow the app to recognise your account.

These technologies are different from advertising trackers used to follow people across unrelated websites.

Knoodle does not intend to use third-party advertising trackers or behavioural advertising to monetise the platform.

The exact technologies used may change as the app develops. Where consent is legally required for a particular technology, it should be obtained before that technology is used.

12. AI and automated processing

Knoodle currently does not use AI to write its code or generate learning content.

There are no AI-powered learning features currently in the application.

We believe technology should be used when it genuinely improves the learning experience, not simply because a feature can be described as AI-powered.

If AI is introduced in the future, we will need to assess what information the feature requires and whether any data would be transmitted to an external provider.

Depending on the feature, this could require changes to the Privacy Policy and additional information for users.

We do not intend to send private study material to an AI provider without a clear, appropriate reason and suitable privacy safeguards.

The current absence of AI features does not mean that no automated software processes information. Authentication, database operations, and saving progress are ordinary technical functions that do not require generative AI.

13. Data retention

Knoodle keeps personal information for as long as necessary to provide the service and fulfil the purposes described in this policy.

For active accounts, this generally means retaining the information needed to provide account access, store study material, and maintain learning progress.

The exact retention period can differ depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is held.

When data is no longer needed, it should be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable law.

Some technical logs or backup copies may remain temporarily after information is deleted from the main application database. The duration depends on the systems and retention arrangements involved.

Where information must be retained to comply with a legal obligation, that information may be kept for the required period.

We aim not to retain personal information indefinitely without a legitimate reason.

14. Deleting your account and all associated data

You should not have to send an email and wait for someone to manually delete your account just because you want to stop using a learning app.

Knoodle provides an account deletion option in the application's Settings for logged-in users.

When you choose the account deletion option and confirm the action, the deletion process is intended to remove your account and all associated learning data stored by Knoodle.

This includes information such as:

  • Your Knoodle account and associated profile information.
  • Your email address as stored in the account system, subject to the deletion process.
  • Your username and nickname.
  • Your saved study decks and personal learning material.
  • Your saved learning progress.
  • Your exercise results and associated learning records.
  • Other personal information associated with your account that is held by Knoodle and can be deleted.

The intention is that deleting your account also deletes your associated learning data, rather than leaving your study material behind in the database.

What happens after deletion?

Once deletion has been completed, you should no longer be able to log in to the deleted account or recover its deleted learning progress through that account.

Account deletion is intended to be permanent. If you decide to use Knoodle again later, you may need to create a new account and start again.

Certain technical records may remain temporarily in backups or security logs, where applicable. Data that must be retained for legal reasons may also be subject to limited exceptions. Such information should not be retained or used beyond what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

Deletion from the primary database and deletion from every backup system may not happen at exactly the same moment.

If you experience a problem with account deletion or believe some of your information remains accessible, contact kaier.chang@proton.me.

We will review the request and help resolve the issue as appropriate.

You do not need to explain why

You do not need to provide a reason for wanting to delete your account.

You are free to stop using Knoodle whenever you want. Your personal data should not be held hostage to your continued use of the app.

15. Your privacy rights under the GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area and the GDPR applies to the processing of your personal information, you may have several rights.

These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions established by law.

Right of access

You may request confirmation of whether Knoodle processes your personal information and ask for access to that information.

You may also request information about the purposes of processing, categories of data, relevant recipients, and applicable retention periods.

Right to rectification

If personal information associated with your account is inaccurate or incomplete, you may request that it be corrected.

Where possible, you may also be able to correct information directly through the app.

Right to erasure

You may request deletion of your personal information where the legal requirements for erasure are met.

You can also use the account deletion option in Settings to initiate deletion of your Knoodle account and associated learning data.

Right to restriction

In certain circumstances, you may request that the processing of your personal information be restricted.

For example, this may apply while the accuracy of information is being contested or an objection is being considered.

Right to data portability

Where the legal requirements apply, you may request a copy of personal information you have provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

This right applies to particular types of processing and does not cover every category of information in every situation.

Right to object

You may have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests where your particular situation gives rise to grounds for objection.

We will assess such requests in accordance with applicable law.

Right to withdraw consent

Where processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

Right to complain

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority if you believe your personal information is being processed unlawfully.

If you are in Germany, the relevant authority will generally depend on your place of residence or the circumstances of the case.
